The Australia Council for the Arts commissioned a longitudinal study of artists at the start of their careers to understand how artists' careers progress, and the impact that arts grants can have on this progression.

An important focus of the study was the impact on recipients of the Australia Council's ArtStart grants program. ArtStart was created to give financial assistance to recent arts graduates, helping them make the challenging transition from studying to having a professional career in the arts.

The research was conducted by Urbis, an independent interdisciplinary consulting firm. We surveyed young and emerging artists that had applied for a grant in 2008-2010 and followed them for a further two years (2011 and 2012) measuring their career development and grant impacts.

The findings in this exhibition compare ArtStart recipients to non-recipients of Australia Council grants. Differences between these groups will be due to the impact of the grant as well as pre-existing differences.

The exhibition features findings from the research, and the work of recipients of ArtStart who have gone on to further success and development in their careers.
